FRC & FTC scouting

Scouting and match strategy for robotics teams

Scouts log matches on their phones in the stands, even with no venue Wi-Fi. The data ends up on the pit laptop, where coaches plan matches and strategy leads build pick lists.

In the stands

Built for thumbs and two-minute matches. Big buttons for auto, teleop and endgame, and every entry saves on the device first, so a screen lock, an app restart or no signal doesn't cost you a match.

Also on the phone: pit scouting with robot photos, dimensions and drivetrain, plus qualitative notes and ratings.
  1. Scout the matchOne tap per action while you watch the robot, not the form.
  2. Turn it into a QR codeThe finished entry is packed into a single code on screen.
  3. Scan it at the pitThe laptop camera reads it. No Wi-Fi or cell signal involved.

At the pit desk

For drive coaches and strategy leads on a laptop or a big screen. Every scout's entries land in one place, next to schedules and scores pulled from The Blue Alliance and Statbotics.

Match predictor
Compare both alliances for any match using scouted averages, EPA or OPR.
Alliance selection
A draft board with live cross-offs, private pick lists and ranked recommendations.
Exports
CSV or JSON whenever you want the raw data for your own scripts.

Who uses what

For Student Scouts

Focus entirely on the robot on the field without fumbling through complex forms. Large tap targets, clear phase transitions, and offline persistence ensure reliable data collection match after match.

  • Fast, intuitive touch controls designed for rapid competition play.
  • No data loss from screen timeouts, app restarts, or lack of cellular service.
  • High-contrast dark mode tailored for indoor arena lighting conditions.
