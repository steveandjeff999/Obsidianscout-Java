Mobile Scouting Interface

Designed for rapid, low-friction thumb entry during two-minute qualification matches. Operates entirely client-side with local persistence.

  • Tactile Cycle Logging: One-tap buttons for autonomous routines, teleop scoring cycles, defense ratings, and endgame objectives.
  • Offline QR Sync: Encodes full match entries into high-density QR codes for optical camera scanning at the pit desk.
  • Pit Hardware Inspections: Capture robot dimensions, intake mechanisms, drivetrain specifications (Swerve, Tank), and direct camera photos.
  • Input Protection: Form validation and state isolation safeguard in-progress matches against accidental navigation or screen locks.
Pit Station & Strategy Desktop Web Dashboard

Strategy & Analytics

Built for drive coaches, strategy leads, and pit operations on laptops or external displays. Aggregates multi-scout entries into actionable strategy.

  • 3v3 Match Predictor: Simulates alliance matchups using scouted cycle averages, official EPA, and component breakdowns.
  • Alliance Selection Draft Board: Interactive pick-list tracking with live team cross-offs, defense ratings, and weighted scoring models.
  • API Ingestion: Continuous background sync with The Blue Alliance (TBA) and Statbotics for match schedules, official scores, and OPR.
  • Data Interoperability: One-click export to CSV and JSON for custom analytics scripts and team archives.
